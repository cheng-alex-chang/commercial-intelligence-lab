How to read this briefing

Spend movement describes advertiser delivery. It is not platform revenue, a churn probability, or a recoverable-revenue estimate. Account counts and amounts are fictional. The data ends on ; this page is a historical sample, not a current operational report.

Comparison scope, rules, and source evidence

Two adjacent seven-day windows. Accounts with any missing delivery are excluded from both portfolio totals; known partial amounts appear only in the data follow-up. Inactive days remain zero. Pacing uses total spend divided by total plan.

Owner-review rule: prior spend ≥ $50,000; decline ≥ 20% and ≥ $10,000; complete evidence; positive plans; current plan ≥ 90% of prior; pacing decline ≥ 10 percentage points. Rank by observed dollar decline; capacity is five by default. These are provisional learning parameters.
